Aspirin and heparin after acute ischaemic stroke: a reanalysis of the International Stroke Trial

19,435 patients randomised in 36 countries between 1991 and 1996, in a 2 by 2 factorial design: aspirin or no aspirin, and heparin (low dose, medium dose or none). This page reproduces the trial's main results from the patient level data in R, then adds a benefit and harm comparison by heparin dose, survival models, corrected subgroup tests, sensitivity analyses for missing data and a cross validated prognostic model. Aspirin effect by subgroup

Death or dependency at 6 months, risk difference per 100 with 95% interval. p values test for interaction. After Holm correction for 8 factors every aspirin p value is 1.00.

Survival to 6 months

Kaplan Meier estimates by aspirin allocation, with 95% intervals. The adjusted hazard ratio is in the summary above.

Prognostic model calibration

Logistic model on baseline factors, 5 fold cross validated. Predicted against observed risk of death or dependency by decile.

Randomisation check

Standardised mean differences between arms. Under 0.1 is balanced.
